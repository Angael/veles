# Veles

Veles is a private personal tracking app for recording food, diary notes, recipes, and body-weight progress.

## Language

**Weight Entry**:
A dated record of a user's body weight that owns any Progress Photos captured for that weight.
_Avoid_: Weight record, check-in

**Progress Photo**:
A highly sensitive photo attached to exactly one Weight Entry. It never exists as an independent gallery item.
_Avoid_: Weight photo, gallery photo

# Popular food seed sources

Nutrition is per **100 g**, converted to integer hundredths without changing USDA values.
Source: [USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy, April 2018 CSV](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_csv_2018-04.zip).
The FDC IDs below identify the exact records used (energy 1008, protein 1003,
fat 1004, carbohydrate 1005). These are representative quick-log foods, not
claims about a particular restaurant, brand, or Polish recipe. Actual recipes,
frying oil uptake, toppings and sauces vary; use a label or recipe for precision.

Serving sizes are deliberately approximate edible defaults, not source nutrition
servings. Drinks also use grams, matching the existing catalog; their defaults
approximate familiar cup/can/bottle sizes, not exact millilitre conversions.
Coffee and tea have no milk or sugar. Pancakes are American-style placuszki,
not thin naleśniki. Nuggets use the generic breaded/fried boneless chicken record.
Soup records are diluted, ready-to-eat, not condensed. Battered chicken wing portions
are edible meat and skin, without bones. Coleslaw and potato salad include dressing;
they are not plain cabbage or potatoes. Soy drink is the USDA original/vanilla blend
and contains sugar; it is not an unsweetened soy drink. Coconut milk is the thick
canned cooking product, not a diluted drinking beverage. Beer and wine energy includes
alcohol and therefore is not expected to match a protein/fat/carbohydrate-only
Atwater calculation.

## Alcoholic drinks

Spirit strength uses alcohol by volume (US proof divided by two). The generic
80-proof distilled-spirit record is used as a representative unflavored gin,
not sweetened or flavored gin. Light beer means lower-calorie beer, not alcohol-free
beer. Cocktail entries are representative ready-to-drink recipes; mixers and
sweeteners vary. Coffee cream liqueur is the generic USDA 34-proof record,
not a claim about a specific brand. Default portions remain **grams**, not ml;
a 40 g spirit portion is not an exact 40 ml shot. Alcohol contributes energy
that is not captured in the protein/fat/carbohydrate fields. Never recalculate
these entries' kcal from those three macros alone.

## Derived hot dog estimate

**Hot dog, plain, no sauces / Hot dog bez sosów**: 50 g of boiled frankfurter
(FDC 171633) plus 50 g of plain hamburger/hotdog roll (FDC 172796), default
100 g. Each per-100-g nutrient is the arithmetic mean of those two USDA records,
rounded to two decimal places. No ketchup, mustard, mayonnaise or vegetables
are included. This is explicitly a composite estimate, not an official USDA
hot dog record.

## Applying the catalog

The worker syncs the catalog each time it starts, so every deploy applies it.
The sync inserts seeds whose `seedKey` is missing and deletes rows whose key
is in `retiredFoodProductSeedKeys`. It does not change existing rows, so user
edits stay. Food logs keep their own nutrition values when a product is deleted.
Run `pnpm db:seed` to sync the development database by hand.

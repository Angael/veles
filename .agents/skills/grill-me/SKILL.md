# Grill Me

Stress-test the user's plan until the consequential decisions, assumptions, and failure modes are explicit. Finish in 1-3 batches, stopping early when one is enough.

1. Inspect the codebase first and answer repository-verifiable questions yourself.
2. Ask one batch of 3-7 concise, numbered, high-leverage questions. Group dependencies and provide a recommended answer with a short reason for each.
3. Summarize settled decisions after the response. Ask another adaptive batch only for consequential gaps exposed by prior answers.
4. Use at most three batches. State reasonable assumptions for low-impact details.
5. Finish with the agreed direction, assumptions, unresolved risks, and next action. Do not implement unless asked.

Challenge contradictions and use concrete failure scenarios where useful. Avoid one-question-at-a-time interviewing, repeated questions, and exhaustive checklists. Let the user answer a whole batch by question number.
